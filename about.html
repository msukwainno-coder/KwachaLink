About us

Making cross-border payments easier to connect

KwachaLink brings people together around real payment needs—whether buying goods abroad, paying suppliers or settling eligible international services.

Our purpose

Many people can find what they want to buy internationally, but struggle to access the payment method or currency required by a seller.

KwachaLink is designed to connect those buyers with people who may be able to help arrange a payment. Users can post requests, share payment assistance offers and discuss terms before deciding whether to proceed.

🎯

Our mission

Help people discover useful cross-border payment connections with clear information and responsible practices.

🌍

Our markets

Begin with China, the United Arab Emirates, South Africa, the United States and Tanzania, while allowing other destinations.

🛡️

Our principles

Transparency, user safety, respect for payment-provider rules and careful handling of personal information.
