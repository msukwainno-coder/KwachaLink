Frequently asked questions

What is KwachaLink?

It connects people who need help arranging an international payment with people who may be able to provide payment assistance.

Does KwachaLink send the money for me?

The initial service focuses on connecting users. Integrated payment services may be added later, subject to provider availability and applicable requirements.

How much is buyer membership?

The planned buyer membership is MK 4,999 per month. Online membership payment must be integrated before it can be collected through the website.

Is every payment method allowed?

No. Providers, buyers and payment methods must comply with the rules of the relevant banks, platforms, countries and applicable laws. Do not attempt to bypass restrictions.

What should I do if someone pressures me?

Pause the transaction, do not share codes or credentials, keep records of the conversation and seek help from your bank or the relevant payment provider if needed.
