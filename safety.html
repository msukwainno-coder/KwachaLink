Your safety matters

Connect with care

Healthy connections begin with respect, consent, privacy, and the freedom to say no.

✓

Community guidelines

  • Be respectful and honest. Harassment, threats, hate, and coercion are not acceptable.
  • Respect boundaries and consent. No one owes you a reply, photo, meeting, or relationship.
  • Do not impersonate others, scam members, or request money under false pretences.
  • Share only content you own or have permission to share.
  • Members must be adults aged 18 or older.
⌑

Meet safely

  • Choose a public place for a first meeting and arrange your own transport.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Keep control of your phone, money, and personal belongings.
  • Do not share passwords, verification codes, financial details, or your home address too soon.
  • Trust your instincts. Leave or ask for help if you feel unsafe.
🔒

Privacy and photos

Use a public name rather than your private legal name. Think carefully before sharing intimate images or identifying details. You can ask for a photo to be removed, but never assume a recipient cannot copy or share it.

In production, private photos need authenticated, authorized backend access. Hiding a link in the frontend is not security.

⚑

Report or block

Use these demo actions to preview the interface. No report reaches a moderation team and blocking only hides the sample item in this page.

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person.

Support and help

For a live platform, add a monitored support channel, response expectations, appeal process, and a clear policy for reports involving threats, harassment, impersonation, or suspected exploitation.
